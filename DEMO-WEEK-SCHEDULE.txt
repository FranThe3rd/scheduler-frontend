Bethlehem Dairy Store — demo weekly schedule (mock app data)
================================================================

Note for anyone on this repo
----------------------------
The first commits had a lot of LLM, DNA, and photo/OCR ideas. We are working backwards on purpose now.
Basics first: roster, weekly grid, edit shifts, copy a week, print or export PDF for employees.
Backend for Mike as admin is next. Smart features can wait.

Not sure we need DNA or LLM for v1. Mike can build schedules in the app and PDF them out.
ChatGPT was bad at reading pictures of the paper schedule anyway.
Why take pics if he can just make them here?

If you have thoughts on what the app must do before we add anything fancy, say so.
